Superman Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-11-22-alpha3

Not legal advice. This notice is written in plain English to describe what the software actually does. The owner should have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before the app is distributed widely.

The short version

  • Superman runs on your computer. Your data stays on your computer. The provider does not receive it.
  • No telemetry and no analytics. Superman does not send usage data, crash reports or your content to the provider.
  • It uses your own AI keys. Text you generate is sent to the AI company whose key you added (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic). Their privacy terms apply.
  • It posts by driving a browser signed in to your own accounts. Those platforms see this activity as you.
  • Updates are on by default if your build has updates enabled, as the official alpha releases do. About every 6 hours the app checks the provider's public release files on GitHub (run by GitHub, Inc.). GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web visit. No account, content or usage information is sent. App updates install only after you confirm; engine updates are applied the next time Superman starts. You can turn updates off (see "Your choices" below).
  • Superman is only for people 18 or older.

Who is responsible: Sai Teja Uppula, an individual trading as The Social Algorithm (not a registered company), in India ("the provider"). Contact: team@thesocialalgorithm.work.

Because Superman keeps your data on your own device, the provider does not collect, store or process your personal data through the app. You control that data. This notice explains what the app stores on your computer and which outside services it talks to.

Network requests the app makes

1. Your AI provider. When you generate, review or check drafts, Superman sends the relevant text to the provider you configured: prompts, research excerpts, your own past posts and style notes used as context, and, for some checks, images such as slide designs. It also lists available models and tests that a key works. Which provider is used depends on the keys you add. Review that provider's terms: some providers keep or review API traffic for a period. 2. Research sources. To find topics, Superman reads public pages and feeds: for example Hacker News, arXiv, Reddit, Hugging Face papers, and a built-in list of technology and privacy news feeds, plus any article pages it is researching. If a GitHub username is configured, it reads that account's public activity from GitHub's API. These sites can see your IP address and a standard browser-style request, like any web visit. 3. Social platforms. Superman opens Chrome windows controlled by the app and signs in as you to read and post on X, LinkedIn, Threads and Instagram. It may also read your own timeline, comments and post statistics to learn what works. Instagram comment discovery may use a public Instagram library. The platforms can see this activity. 4. Updates (on by default in the official alpha releases). Only if your build has updates enabled, as the official alpha releases do by default: shortly after Superman starts, and then about every 6 hours while it runs, the app fetches small signed update files from tejalogs.github.io/superman-releases and, when a newer version exists, downloads it from github.com/tejalogs/superman-releases, which redirects to GitHub's download servers (release-assets.githubusercontent.com or objects.githubusercontent.com). If your build has updates turned off, or you turn them off, Superman makes no update requests. These hosts are run by GitHub, Inc. (owned by Microsoft), a third party; GitHub can see your IP address, the time, the files requested and a basic request header from the app, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that. No account, content, key or usage information is sent. Where that location is the provider's public release repository on GitHub (github.com/tejalogs/superman-releases), the requests go to GitHub and the provider does not receive them or any report of who checked. What is downloaded: engine updates (the content engine and interface) are downloaded in the background and applied automatically the next time Superman starts; if a new engine fails to start, the previous one is restored. App updates (the whole app, on macOS and on Windows) are downloaded in the background and installed only after you confirm "Restart and update". The Windows .zip copy and a Mac copy that was not moved to Applications do not update the app itself. Every update is checked against the provider's release signature before it is used. To turn updates off, see "Your choices" below. 5. Telegram (optional). If you add a Telegram bot token and chat ID, approval and alert messages (such as a draft awaiting approval) are sent to that bot. Off unless you set it up. 6. Owner-only publishing. Some developer features can push pages to Git repositories belonging to the app's owner. They are not meant for other users and refuse to run on client profiles. They need git credentials that you would have to provide yourself.

The app's own interface is served from 127.0.0.1 (your own computer) only.

Browser mode (preview)

Browser mode shows real web pages inside the Superman window, next to the Superman panel.

  • Superman reads a page only when you press a button (Read, Research or Save), and only that page. It does not read pages in the background.
  • It never reads banking, email, payment or password-manager sites, or sign-in pages, even if you ask.
  • "Research this page" sends the page text to your AI provider (as in point 1 above) and may visit pages it links to (as in point 2).
  • "Reply to this" sends the post's text and the first few replies under it to your AI provider to draft a reply. The draft waits in your Approve queue; nothing is posted until you approve it.
  • Web pages cannot reach the app: they get no access to your files, keys or Superman's data, and camera, microphone, location and notification requests are refused. Downloads from web pages are blocked.

What Superman does not do

  • No telemetry, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data by the provider. The provider does not receive it.
  • No sending of your keys or cookies anywhere except to the service they belong to.
  • Diagnostics are copy/save only. Settings has a "Copy diagnostics" and "Save diagnostics" button. It builds a text report on request (versions, system info, connection state, recent errors, a redacted log tail) and hands it to you. It is never sent automatically. Secrets are redacted, but read it before you share it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Stop posting: use Settings pause/recovery controls, or quit the app.
  • Turn updates off: create a file named update-config.json in the Superman application data folder (on macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Superman; on Windows: %APPDATA%\Superman) containing {"updates": "off"}, then restart Superman. The app then makes no update requests at all. Delete the file to turn updates back on. With updates off you will not get fixes, including security fixes, until you download a new version yourself.
  • Remove a key: Settings, Connections. This deletes it from the keychain.
  • Log out of a platform: delete that platform's browser folder listed above, or sign out in its window.
  • Delete everything: quit the app, then delete the Superman application data folder (including data, engine-updates, shell-updates and any .env), the browser login folders listed above, and the Superman entries in your keychain (Keychain Access on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, search for "Superman"). Then uninstall the app. Since nothing is sent to the provider, there is nothing to delete on a server. Deleting the data folder also removes your record of accepting the terms, so you will be asked again.
  • Content already posted to a platform, and anything an AI provider received, is held by those companies. Use their tools to delete it.
